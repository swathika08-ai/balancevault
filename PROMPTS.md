# PROMPTS.md: how I used AI on this project

> This file is only worth marks if it is TRUE. Fill it in yourself, as you go, in your own words.
> Delete these instructions once you start. The challenge asks for at least 5 dated entries, and at
> least 2 about authorisation, access control or the audit trail.

## Honest summary (write this yourself)

Which tools did you use, and for what? State plainly which parts were generated by the AI and which
you wrote or changed. Example of the kind of sentence that is fine: "The first version of the backend
was generated by Claude; I then read every route, ran the tests, and changed X and Y."

## Entry format

### YYYY-MM-DD: short title
- **Asked for:** what you typed or asked the tool to do
- **Tool produced:** what it gave you
- **What was wrong / what I checked:** a real problem, risk or question you found by reading or running it
- **What I did instead:** your fix, with a link to the commit
